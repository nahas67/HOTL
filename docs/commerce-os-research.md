# Commerce operating system: product and protocol research

Reviewed 2026-09-09. This is a bounded study of official documentation for master-prompt sections 93–94. It informs design; it is neither an integration certification nor evidence that HOTL has implemented the capabilities below. Product pages describe vendor capabilities, not independently measured performance. Workflow gaps and design implications are explicitly our inferences. The wider category study and each provider's implementation-specific review remain separate work.

## Representative product study

| Product | Documented strength | Workflow gap / implication for HOTL (inference) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify Flow | Accessible trigger/condition/action workflows connect store events and other apps. Run history supports resource-ID searches, errors and retry filters. Completed runs are retained for 14 days. [Flow reference](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-flow/reference), [run monitoring](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-flow/monitor). | Keep the inspectable event → condition → action explanation. Preserve HOTL's own durable business audit and provider IDs rather than relying on a partner's run retention. Link an intervention to the exact affected order/product and failed step. |
| Linnworks | Central catalog/inventory records, channel stock updates, buffers, listing suppression, and replenishment prompts address overselling. The product page describes sync in under ten minutes; its marketing also uses more immediate language. [Inventory management](https://www.linnworks.com/solutions/inventory-management/). | Show measured freshness and reconciliation status per channel; do not turn marketing language into a transactional consistency guarantee. A campaign decision needs channel-available inventory, reservations and inbound stock, not just one stock number. |
| Gorgias | Its commerce AI handles support and shopping conversations. Actions are configurable and opt-in, with handover to humans and reports; configuration includes knowledge and skills. [AI Agent explained](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/ai-agent-explained-497772). | Cross-app actions and human handover already exist. Differentiate through a company-wide decision record: refund exposure, supplier quality and retention context together. Keep a structured exception queue, with a clear responsible human and resumption outcome. |
| Klaviyo | Event/data-triggered flows support draft, manual and live action states. Profile filters are reevaluated before sending. Previously queued manual messages remain subject to review even after switching to live. [Getting started with flows](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002774932). | Separate future autonomy settings from already pending approvals. Reevaluate eligibility immediately before execution. Join campaign economics to consent, order/refund status and inventory so a change in one department can stop an inappropriate downstream action. |
| Shippo | The API offers rating and label workflows; tracking, transaction and batch webhooks report lifecycle changes. Test keys support test labels without purchase charges, but test rates can differ from live rates. [API overview](https://docs.goshippo.com/api-reference/overview), [authentication](https://docs.goshippo.com/docs/guides_general/authentication/), [webhooks](https://docs.goshippo.com/tracking/webhooks). | Separate quote, purchase, label readiness and delivery. A provider request acceptance is not shipment completion. Present shipping exceptions beside the order's payment/support context, with a safe retry/reconcile action and test/live evidence label. |
| Inventory Planner | Replenishment combines forecasts, stock, lead-time demand and expected incoming stock. Recommendations include purchase timing and estimated lost sales; lead times and stock cover must be configured. [Replenishment](https://help.inventory-planner.com/en/articles/589017-replenishment). | Separate a demand estimate from a purchase recommendation and from spending authorization. Put data freshness, assumptions and incoming-PO dates next to proposed quantity/cost. Supplier evidence and cash constraints must be evaluated before committing a PO. |

The reviewed products demonstrate substantial automation. These sources do not establish that any particular vendor lacks a feature or that customers routinely lose data. The cross-tool gap is an architectural inference: independently configured tools have different identifiers, permissions, timing and completion semantics. HOTL should make those boundaries explicit and reconcile them around shared business entities.

## Owner workflow decisions derived from the study

1. **Start with work requiring attention.** Group pending approvals, stale connectors, delivery exceptions and failed operations by business impact and urgency. Show affected entities and the next valid action, not just departmental counters.
2. **Make one decision record reusable across views.** Include proposal, reason, source timestamps, policy version, requested authority, economic impact, provider operation ID and current outcome. The order, customer, supplier and campaign views should open the same record.
3. **Separate decision from execution.** Distinguish proposed, awaiting owner, denied, authorized, executing, unknown outcome, reconciled success and failure. An approved decision can still be blocked by changed facts or degraded connectivity. Never make retrying a historical authorization create fresh authority.
4. **Represent connector health as evidence.** Distinguish configured credentials, verified authentication, granted capabilities, last successful sync, webhook health, rate limiting and unresolved reconciliation. A green connection alone does not prove a financial write can safely execute.
5. **Keep intervention controls contextual.** Explain what approve, modify, reject, pause and retry will affect. Show scope and authority expiration. A changed autonomy rule should not silently release previously pending actions.
6. **Measure outcomes across functions.** Pair acquisition and retention metrics with margin, refund exposure, stock coverage and fulfillment reliability. Label simulated, observed and estimated values independently.

These are design recommendations, not implementation claims.

## Protocol research and support boundaries

| Protocol | Verified official snapshot | Design implication / caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UCP | The official release announcement identifies **2026-08-25**. The versioned specification defines commerce discovery, services/capabilities, payment handlers and transport bindings. It uses exact minor-unit amounts and capability negotiation. [Release announcement](https://ucp.dev/documentation/announcements/), [versioned specification](https://ucp.dev/2026-08-25/specification/overview/). | Pin an implemented snapshot and advertise only tested capabilities. REST, MCP and A2A bindings are not interchangeable payloads. Discovery is not merchant authentication, delegated spending authority, payment-processor enablement or proof of conformance. Maintain internal policy checks independently. |
| MCP | The official latest URL redirects to **2026-07-28**. This revision removes the initialization handshake and protocol sessions, adds per-request version/capability metadata and `server/discover`, and moves asynchronous tasks into an extension. [Specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28), [revision changes](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog). | Do not assume an older SDK or initialize-based example supports this revision. Record client, SDK and transport versions, validate actual wire behavior and fail unsupported negotiation. Tool discovery provides no authority to bypass HOTL permissions; tool descriptions and returned content are untrusted inputs. |
| A2A | Official documentation lists **1.0.0** as the released specification; the wire version is **1.0**. Agent Cards advertise interfaces and security. v1.0 changes message shapes, operation conventions and enum representation from 0.3. [Specification](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/specification/), [v1.0 changes](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/whats-new-v1/), [release](https://a2a-protocol.org/dev/blog/2026/03/12/a2a-protocol-ships-v10-production-ready-standard-for-agent-to-agent-communication/). | Verify remote identity, interface version, tenant and optional capabilities before delegation. Use task state and cancellation semantics, not a generic chat completion. An Agent Card alone is not operational support; conformance and remote interoperability tests must be recorded separately. |

A protocol can describe an integration surface while a provider still requires account setup, API approval, OAuth consent and capability-specific enablement. Do not display “supported” based solely on a metadata endpoint or a standards logo.

## Example integration intake: Shippo

This example shows why each provider needs its own verified intake before adapter implementation. It is not a completed adapter review.

| Area | Verified fact and required follow-through |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication and platform setup | Direct API keys use `ShippoToken`; platform OAuth uses Bearer credentials. OAuth setup requires Shippo contact and preconfigured callbacks. The documented OAuth scope is currently `*`, and access tokens do not expire. Keep these broad provider credentials behind the deterministic execution service. [OAuth guide](https://docs.goshippo.com/o-auth-integrations/o-auth). |
| Version | `Shippo-API-Version` controls request and webhook behavior. Record the exact version selected for the account and contract tests; do not infer it from an example date. An account upgrade is not documented as reversible. [Versioning](https://docs.goshippo.com/api-concepts/api-versioning). |
| Quotas | Limits differ by resource, verb and test/live mode. For example, the published transaction POST limits are 500 live / 50 test per minute; list GET limits are 50 live / 10 test. Treat 429 as a constrained retry condition. Recheck applicable limits at implementation. [Rate limits](https://docs.goshippo.com/api-concepts/rate-limits). |
| Events and lag | Webhook acknowledgments should arrive within three seconds. Selected timeout/408/429/5xx failures receive two retries. Tracking updates depend on carrier polling; the guide describes an average within two hours. Persist ingress, deduplicate and reconcile missing events. [Webhook behavior](https://docs.goshippo.com/tracking/webhooks). |
| Event authentication | HMAC is documented but requires setup with Shippo; do not claim it is enabled automatically. The guide also describes IP allowlists and URL tokens. Verify the configured mechanism with received raw payloads before trusting events. [Webhook security](https://docs.goshippo.com/tracking/webhook-security). |
| Sandbox and remaining requirements | Test keys produce uncharged test labels with distinct data and potentially different rates. Carrier-specific capabilities, prohibited goods/operations, account approval, billing and production revocation behavior require explicit review for the chosen use case before activation. [Test/live authentication](https://docs.goshippo.com/docs/guides_general/authentication/). |

For every further provider, record supported operations, auth/scopes, exact API version, quota behavior, webhook verification/replay rules, sandbox differences, platform approvals and prohibited operations with official links and a reviewed date. Missing evidence must remain “unverified,” never become a guessed endpoint or an enabled capability.
